Hello, my name is RMBK. We are currently in Taipei, specifically Wenzhen, to watch a game called "The Insights." Our goal is to create a Taiwanese cyberpunk experience, and we've already established a design team in Taiwan. I'm taking the lead on this project.

This game has received extremely high praise, both in Steam ratings and among Taiwanese players. It's truly excellent.  However, there are a few dissenting opinions, which is puzzling.  I'm not sure why some people dislike it, but overall, the game is undeniably good.  We're still working towards our goals, but I believe this game has great potential despite some minor issues.

Later this year, I'll be working on another project.  Our primary focus is on capturing the authentic Taiwanese experience.  We want to explore what life is like in Taiwan. We have one month to achieve this, and I admit to feeling a little anxious.  I'm also considering taking on another project. We're hoping for the best!

This is their office.  Hi Ted! Hello everyone! It's a bit of a commute from the Taipei area. I'm from the Taipei area, as are most of the team members. My father's side of the family is also from the Taipei area, so I've always been connected to this region. I come from a large family and have a brother.  As a child, I loved writing stories, often focusing on fantastical narratives.

The idea for a Taiwan Cyberpunk project came about naturally. We were discussing potential game ideas with a few companies, and the idea emerged to create and sell a game with this theme.  The concept intrigued me. This is our first attempt at creating a Taiwan Cyberpunk game, and it explores the future of human knowledge and technology.

This focus led to two key story elements.  First, there's a significant shortage of computing power. Second, many jobs have been lost due to automation, leading to widespread unemployment.  In this setting, a company develops technology that uses human minds as computers.  People willingly enter this "business factory," fall asleep for about ten years, and wake up to a large sum of money. This is the basic premise of the story.

I believe this narrative reflects a common sentiment in modern society, where people tend to assume their own perspective is correct, implying others are wrong. This game challenges that assumption and offers a different viewpoint.  We experimented with different approaches to see how they would play out and achieve specific effects within the game world.

This led to the development of a core game mechanic centered around a dream-like experience. The primary space is both a room and an experiment.  The player enters this space, which is essentially a shared dream environment. The goal is to offer players a unique and immersive experience.  I'm still working on the details of this mechanic, but it involves navigating and interacting within this dream world.  We want to ensure it integrates seamlessly with the cyberpunk setting and contributes to the overall narrative.
