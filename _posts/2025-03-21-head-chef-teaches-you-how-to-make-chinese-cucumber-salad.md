**Wang Gang:** Hello everyone, I am Wang Gang. 

Today, I would like to share the recipe for a home-cooked dish: Cucumber Salad. 

First, we need to prepare the salad sauce. 

You'll need 15 grams of garlic. Use a knife to press and mince the garlic. Next, prepare 10 grams of capsicum frutescens and chop it up. If you’re not a fan of spicy food, you can skip this step.

Also, prepare a spring onion and chop it. If you enjoy its aroma, you might want to add some coriander as well. 

Now, let's add some seasonings: 2 grams of salt, 1 gram of MSG, 3 grams of soy sauce, and 1 gram of aromatic vinegar to enhance the flavors. Don't forget some sesame oil. Finally, add in 20 grams of homemade red oil. 

Mix all the ingredients together until well combined. 

Now, let's move on to the cucumbers. First, cut off both the top and bottom ends. Then, scratch the surface of the cucumber. This helps reduce the bitter taste and improves the presentation of the dish. 

Using a knife, press down on the cucumber and slice it. Separate the inner part and cut it into similarly sized pieces. Arrange the stuffed cucumber pieces at the bottom of the plate.

The final step is to mix the cucumber with the sauce and place the mixture on the plate. 

Now you have a plate of tasty and refreshing cucumber salad ready to enjoy! 

**Summary:** 
1. To reduce the water content in the cucumber, it’s essential to prepare the sauce before mixing it with the cucumber. 
2. Separating the stuffed cucumber serves the same purpose as mentioned earlier.
