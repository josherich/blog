All controls & how to play

P3: J / L, I jump, K use. P4: F / H, T jump, G use. P5–P10: gamepads, or select a cat with Tab and use P1 controls. Gamepad: left stick or D-pad, A jump, B use.

Stand on friends to jump higher. Push numbered barriers and blocks together. Use ↓ / S at doors, signs, balls and pipes. R restarts; Esc pauses. Changing crew size restarts the stage.
