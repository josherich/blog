How to paint 用法

Object brushes (B). Pick an object from the inventory and paint a stroke. Pattern brushes (pines, bare trees, slender trees, bamboo…) strew objects along your stroke in their own pattern; a click puts down just one. Stroke brushes (distant mountains, branches, waterfalls, ink) turn the stroke itself into the object. Branches grow: start a new stroke on the selected branch and it becomes a limb of it. Stamps (moon, ducks, people, buildings) are placed one by one — drag to size them.

Seeds. Every object keeps the strokes you drew and a seed. Change the seed (or press R) for another variation of the same strokes.

Select (V). Move, scale with the corners, rotate with the round handle. Properties on the right: brush settings, colour from the traditional pigments, opacity, blending and effects (ink waver, bleed, dry brush, fade into mist, distance…).

Mist eraser (E). Paints the layer's mask so ink dissolves softly into the silk; switch to Restore to bring it back.

Seals (S) and inscriptions (T). Design a seal — characters, font, 白文/朱文, shape, frame, wear — and click to stamp it. Upload your own seal-script font under Canvas → Fonts.

Files. Export a PNG or SVG. Save writes a .shanshui project file with the full edit history; Open it to carry on, undo included. The session is also kept in this browser.

Keys. Space drag to pan, ⌘+wheel to zoom, ⌘0 fit, ⌘Z/⇧⌘Z undo/redo, ⌘D duplicate, Del delete, [ ] order, arrows nudge.

物笔 (B)。从物件栏中选一样，画一笔。排布类笔刷（松、寒林、杂树、竹……）会沿着笔画按各自的方式散布物件；单击则只放一个。笔画类笔刷（远山、枝、瀑布、墨）则直接把笔画变成物件。枝可以生长：在选中的枝上另起一笔，就会长成它的分枝。印章类（月、鸭、人物、屋宇）逐个放置——拖动可调整大小。

种子。每个物件都保存着你画的笔画和一个种子。更换种子（或按 R），同样的笔画就会有另一种变化。

选择 (V)。移动；拖角点缩放；拖圆形手柄旋转。右侧的属性面板：笔刷设置、传统颜料设色、不透明度、混合模式与效果（墨晕、洇、飞白、云遮、远……）。

留白 (E)。在图层的蒙版上作画，让墨色柔和地融入绢中；切换到「恢复」可将其找回。

印章 (S) 与题款 (T)。设计印章——印文、字体、白文/朱文、形状、边框、残损——然后单击钤印。可在「画布 → 字体」中上传你自己的篆书字体。

文件。可导出 PNG 或 SVG。保存会写出带有完整编辑历史的 .shanshui 项目文件；打开它即可继续创作，包括撤销。当前会话也会保存在本浏览器中。

快捷键。空格 拖动平移，⌘+滚轮缩放，⌘0 适合窗口，⌘Z/⇧⌘Z 撤销/重做，⌘D 复制，Del 删除，[ ] 调整层次，方向键微移。
